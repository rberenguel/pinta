Pinta 🪮
Structured outline editor
Click on lines to add new child lines
Hover on lines to resize (top)
Hover on lines to drag on parent line (bottom)
Hover on lines to delete it and any children (X)
Text stuff
🔗 While editing text you can add/remove links with Cmd/Ctrl k
You can add new lines with shift enter. And (un)center with Ctrl-c
You can resize the font with Ctrl , and Ctrl .
And bold with Ctrl b
Press ? to get help
You can resize in reverse to swap direction
Post its
Hold on an empty space to add a Post it
Change its color while editing
Ctrl y: yellow
Ctrl r: red
Ctrl b: blue
Ctrl g: green
Ctrl t: translucent
Ctrl . and Ctrl , also resize the font on post its
Post its can be dragged
Drawing
You can draw arrows, rectangles and "highlights"
Press a and drag to draw an arrow
R for rectangles
You can switch colors by pressing c and then
r for red
b for blue
g for green
I'll add more
